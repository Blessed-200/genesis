1) CLIFFORD_NORM_WEIGHTS[i]=REVERSE_SIGN[grade(i)]*SIGNATURE_TABLE[i].

INEVITABLE_SOLUTION: Add active-mask aware norm routine for SparseCliffordVector and keep dense version for SIMD-friendly dense contexts.
WHY_BRIEF: Reduces unnecessary loads/mults when sparsity is high.

}
